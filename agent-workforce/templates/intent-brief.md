# Intent Brief — how the Director assigns work

Copy this when briefing an agent. Never say "make some posts" — give the spec, then let the agent ask first. *(operating system → "How to brief an agent")*

---

**Agent:** &lt;which agent&gt;   **Date:** &lt;YYYY-MM-DD&gt;   **Serves:** &lt;① / ② / ③ / —&gt;

- **GOAL** — &lt;the outcome, and the *why* behind it&gt;
- **SUCCESS** — &lt;how we'll know it's done: the metric or the check&gt;
- **INTEGRATION** — may touch: &lt;tools / files / DBs&gt;  ·  may **NOT** touch: &lt;DB writes · `.env` · publish · deploy&gt;
- **RESOURCES** — &lt;brand-kit · playbook · examples · templates&gt;

→ **Grill me:** *"Ask me clarifying questions before you start."*

---

### Example
**Agent:** Growth Lead · **Serves:** ①
- **GOAL** — 3 build-in-public posts from this week's progress, because ① is flat.
- **SUCCESS** — 3 drafts ready for my approval; each tied to one niche community.
- **INTEGRATION** — may touch: Notion content calendar, Ahrefs. May **NOT**: publish, send.
- **RESOURCES** — `brand-kit/`, last week's top post.
→ Grill me first.
