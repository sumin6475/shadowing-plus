# Daily Driver — "what should I do today?"

*The keystone of this whole system. Open a fresh Cowork session, point Claude at `agent-workforce/`, and ask: **"what should I do today?"***

## Notion pane — the single source of state
- 🚀 **Launch hub:** https://app.notion.com/p/384c0c9f8ce481efa787dfbfa3ad4140
- **Launch Checklist:** https://app.notion.com/p/4d13452e382e4b0baa8a630a722b7b95
- **Post Tracker:** https://app.notion.com/p/9697d0d25b02420f88c8e9b78b931602
- **Experiments / Examples:** https://app.notion.com/p/ef4c702329514a539cff36a9594480fb
- **Insights & Decisions:** https://app.notion.com/p/dff932679576440a9c1d2ef22793dba1

## What Claude reads
1. **Notion → Launch Checklist** — next + overdue items.
2. **Notion → Post Tracker** — anything `Needs OK` or due today.
3. **Notion → Experiments** — results waiting for your call.
4. **Notion → Insights & Decisions** — open decisions.
5. The relevant role pack (`growth/`, `brand/`, `dev/`) for the topic at hand.

## What Claude gives back (always this shape)
> **① TODAY — pick 1–3:** the highest-leverage moves for the launch.
> **② NEEDS YOUR 5-MIN VERIFY:** ≤3 specific items (a draft to OK, one decision). Each actionable right now.
> **③ ONE DECISION (if any):** stated plainly, with your options.
> **④ HANDLED / PARKED:** what's moving without you — ignore it.

No process dumps. If nothing needs you, it says so.

## Routing (which pack to wear)
- "where/what do I post", launch plan, posts → **growth/**
- landing, demo, visuals, "did this style work" → **brand/**
- "deploy", "it broke", "is this safe to ship" → **dev/**

## Memory
Say **"remember this"** and Claude writes it to the right pack's `memory.md` + the Notion Insights log. Nothing is saved otherwise.

## Optional
A daily scheduled task can pre-write this brief into Notion each morning so it's waiting for you. (Say the word and I'll set it up.)
