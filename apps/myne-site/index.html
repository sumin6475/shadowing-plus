Free. Your speaking recordings stay on your phone.

From saved to spoken, in three moves.

Most saved phrases never get said. Myne is built around the step in between.

1 · Save

Keep phrases that are yours

Type or paste a phrase, or take a photo of a page and pick the line you want.

  • Optional AI fill adds the meaning and a usage note in your language
  • English, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Russian
2 · Practice

Three small steps per phrase

Say it along with the audio, hear it in real videos, then write a sentence you would really say.

  • Collected → Learning → Ready
  • A short daily list, at the size you choose
3 · Speak

Talk to the mirror

See yourself while you speak. Your words appear as you talk, and hint cards check off the phrases you use.

  • Speaking time, pace and word count afterwards
  • Listen back or go again

Write what you want to say first.

Studio is a simple notebook for speaking. Outline an opening, a body and a closing for a meeting, an interview or a story, then speak it out loud with your outline on screen.

  • Recordings stay on your phone. Speaking audio is never uploaded.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device. No video of the mirror is recorded.
  • AI is optional. It stays off until you turn it on, and the app works without it.
  • Your data is yours. Export your phrases or delete your account any time in Settings.
