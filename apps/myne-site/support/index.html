Common questions

Talk does not hear me, or there is no transcript

Talk needs the microphone and speech recognition. On your iPhone open Settings → Apps → Myne and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Speech is turned into text on your phone. Myne currently understands English.

The mirror is black

The mirror uses the front camera. Turn on Camera for Myne in Settings → Apps → Myne.

Myne only shows the camera image live. It does not record video.

“Fill” or reading text from a photo does not work

Those are AI features and stay off until you allow them. In the app open Profile → Settings & account → Privacy and turn on “Allow OpenAI processing”.

Without it you can still add phrases by hand and listen with your device’s voice.

Where are my recordings?

On your phone only. They are never uploaded, so they do not move to a new phone and they are removed if you delete the app.

Transcripts and stats are saved to your account.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password?” and follow the link in the email. If the email does not arrive, check spam, then write to us.

How do I take a copy of my phrases?

In the app open Profile → Settings & account → Export my phrases.
