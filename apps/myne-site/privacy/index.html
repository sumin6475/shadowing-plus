Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Myne (“the app”) is an independently run speaking-practice app for iPhone. This policy explains what we collect, why, what stays on your phone, and who processes your data. We try not to collect more than the product needs. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your voice Recordings stay on your phone Speaking audio is never uploaded. Speech becomes text on your device.
AI Only with your permission Nothing is sent to our AI provider unless you allow it, and you can turn it off any time.
No tracking No ads, no selling data We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and a securely hashed password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Profile. The name, speaking goal, and optional profile photo you add, plus app preferences such as your first language, English level, and daily goal. Your profile photo is stored with Supabase and served from a web address that is hard to guess but not password-protected, so anyone who has that exact link can view it.
  • Learning content. The phrases, speaking notes, practice transcripts, and practice history you save. These are private to your account.
  • Usage analytics. Basic product interactions and crash diagnostics (see “Usage analytics” below).

Your voice and Mirror mode

  • Practice recordings made in the iPhone app stay on your device. They are never uploaded to us.
  • Speech is turned into text on your device by iOS speech recognition. The resulting transcript is saved to your account so you can review it.
  • Mirror mode shows your camera image live on your screen only. Myne does not record or save video of your mirror practice.
  • You can delete any speaking session, with its recording, from your Profile (swipe it under Recent sessions).

AI features (only with your permission)

Some features in the app use AI. To run them, the content involved is sent to our AI provider, OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., United States), through Myne’s own server, and the result comes back to you. Nothing is sent unless you allow it on the consent screen, on the “Turn on AI processing” sheet shown inside a feature, or in Settings → Privacy. One switch covers every AI feature, and you can turn it off there at any time. With it off, AI features stay off and nothing is sent.

What each feature sends, and only when you use it:

  • Speaking review. The transcript of the saved speaking session you are reviewing, your speaking focus (the goals you wrote), your English level, and the language you want the review in. If you answer a follow-up question or try a sentence again, that answer or the new transcript is sent too.
  • Ask. The messages you type to Ask and, when you ask from a note or a practice session, that note’s text or that session’s transcript so the answer fits what you are working on.
  • Phrase meaning and usage. The phrase you are saving and the context you typed with it.
  • Reading text from a photo. The photo you pick. It is used for that capture only and is not stored by Myne.
  • Phrase search. The text of your saved phrases, turned into search vectors (“embeddings”) so similar phrases can be found.
  • AI voice. The phrase text, to generate its spoken pronunciation.

What is never sent: your recordings (speaking audio stays on your phone), your camera image, your email address, or your password.

How OpenAI handles it:

  • OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default.
  • OpenAI may keep API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days to provide the service and detect abuse, then deletes them unless the law requires otherwise. See OpenAI’s API data controls and privacy policy.
  • OpenAI processes this content on our behalf, under its API terms, to the same standard of protection described in this policy.
  • AI results (for example a speaking review, or a phrase’s meaning) are saved to your account like the rest of your learning content and are deleted with it.

AI can be wrong. Treat reviews and explanations as practice help, not as professional or authoritative advice.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the app is used and to find crashes, so we can improve it. PostHog receives app events (for example, that a screen was opened or a practice session finished) and crash diagnostics, linked to an internal account ID. We do not send your email, recordings, transcripts, phrase text, photos, or advertising identifiers to PostHog.

Service providers

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run the service:

  • Supabase — sign-in, database, file storage (profile photos), and server functions.
  • OpenAI — our AI provider for the AI features above, only with your permission (United States).
  • PostHog — usage analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage for the spoken pronunciation of your phrases (so it plays instantly next time).
  • Vercel — hosting for this website.
  • ElevenLabs, Groq and Cloudflare R2 — only if you also sign in to our separate web app (shadowing-plus.vercel.app) with the same account and use its clip feature: storage and speech-to-text for audio or video files you upload there. The iPhone app does not use them for this.

Links to other sites

“Open YouGlish” on a phrase opens youglish.com inside the app so you can hear the phrase in real videos. Only the phrase you tapped is sent, as part of the web address, and only when you tap it. That site and the videos it plays have their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile and photo, phrases, notes, transcripts, and saved pronunciation audio are deleted right away and cannot be recovered.
  • You can also remove individual phrases, notes, and recordings in the app at any time.
  • Take a copy: Settings → Export my phrases.
  • Waitlist details are kept until you ask us to remove them or they are no longer needed for launch. Email sumin002@gmail.com to be removed.
  • Otherwise, we keep your data for as long as your account exists.

Security and international processing

Your learning content is protected by your account and is not public. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no online service can be perfectly secure. Our providers may process data in countries other than your own under their applicable safeguards. Please do not add highly sensitive or confidential information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age of digital consent in their country, and they should not use it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Most of this can be done in the app; for anything else, contact sumin002@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes how it handles data, and the date above will show the latest version. If we introduce a feature that shares your content with other people, it will be optional and will ask for your permission first.
